Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the Pena Plaza website may be used.

Information you provide

When you submit a leasing inquiry, the website may collect information such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, business type, space requirements, desired timing, and message.

How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to your inquiry, discuss current or future commercial space, maintain inquiry records, and improve the website and leasing process.

Website analytics

If analytics are enabled, the site may collect general usage information such as pages viewed, device or browser information, referral source, and approximate location derived from network information. Update this section if a specific analytics or advertising service is added.

Sensitive information

Do not submit bank account numbers, payment-card information, Social Security numbers, passwords, or other sensitive financial or identity information through the public leasing form.

Third-party services

The website may use hosting, form-processing, analytics, or other service providers that process information as needed to provide those services. This policy should be reviewed before launch to reflect the services actually used.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the website and its services change.
