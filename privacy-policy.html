Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the Peña Plaza website may be used.

Information you provide

When you submit a leasing inquiry, the website may collect information such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, business type, space requirements, desired timing, building preference, request type, preferred language, and message.

How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to your inquiry, discuss current or future commercial space, maintain inquiry records, and improve the website and leasing process.

Website usage

This website does not include advertising or analytics tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver and maintain the website.

Sensitive information

Do not submit bank account numbers, payment-card information, Social Security numbers, passwords, or other sensitive financial or identity information through the public leasing form.

Third-party services

The site is configured to use Netlify for hosting and processing leasing inquiries. Information submitted through the forms is processed by the hosting provider and made available to the property’s site administrators to handle your inquiry.

Questions about your information

Use the contact form to ask about information you previously submitted. Include only the details needed to identify your inquiry.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the website and its services change.
