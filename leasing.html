Leasing FAQ

Details to discuss before you lease

What are the rent and deposit amounts?

Pricing and deposits are not currently published. Request a quote for the specific building and suite, including any additional charges.

What is the minimum lease term?

Lease lengths must be confirmed for the specific space. Include your preferred term in the inquiry.

Are utilities included?

Confirm which utilities and operating expenses are included and which are billed separately before agreeing to a lease.

Can the space be changed for my business?

Describe the layout, equipment, signage, or other improvements you need. Any modifications and their costs require confirmation for the property.

Is my business type allowed?

Include your intended business use in the inquiry so suitability and any required approvals can be discussed.

How do I arrange a tour?

Select “Request a tour” in the inquiry form, choose a building, and share your preferred days or times. Confirm the appointment and address before visiting.
