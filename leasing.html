Leasing FAQ

Common questions about Pena Plaza space

How do I inquire about leasing at Pena Plaza?

Submit the leasing inquiry form with your business type, approximate space needs, and desired timing. Pena Plaza can then confirm whether appropriate space is available.

Are Pena Plaza lease rates published online?

Rates can be published for individual spaces when they are approved and current. If a rate is not listed, submit a leasing inquiry for verified information.

Can I ask about future Pena Plaza space?

Yes. The leasing inquiry form can be used for both current and upcoming space so prospective tenants can share what they need.

Can tenants pay rent through this website?

Not at this stage. This public website is focused on property information and leasing. A secure tenant portal can be added separately when the payment process is ready.
